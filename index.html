Erdong Xu

I am an applied microeconomist working at the intersection of political economy and economic history.

I study how ideas and economic interests shape political behavior and policy priorities, and how institutional arrangements influence policy implementation and its economic consequences. My work combines newly assembled historical sources and contemporary administrative records with geographic analysis and quasi-experimental methods to examine these questions across different settings.
